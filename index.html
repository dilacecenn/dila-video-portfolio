ABOUT THE ARTIST

Dila
Çeçen.

Dila Çeçen is a visual artist and educator with a bachelor’s degree and a master’s degree in Painting from İnönü University. Her practice and research focus on painting, visual perception, art history, composition and the relationship between architecture and pictorial space.

She has more than seven years of experience in visual arts education, including drawing, painting, perspective and portfolio development. These video studies extend her interest in visual communication into experimental movement and color analysis.
